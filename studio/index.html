Image boundaries in the firmware are not labelled, so offset, width and height are estimates — nudge them until the tiles line up, then click one to replace it. Offset is free-form hex, so you are not limited to the detected regions. Auto re-estimates the stride at wherever you are; ±row shifts by one row so you can walk an image into alignment. Keep alpha byte copies the third byte of every pixel from the original, which preserves icon shapes and anything else encoded there; turn it off only deliberately. Shift+click a tile to replace it from Valeton Suite's own artwork — the same images at far higher resolution than the firmware carries.
Only runs that look like real text and sit among other strings are listed — the firmware is full of printable byte sequences that are not text at all. Edits are written in place; the room column is the hard limit in bytes. Press Enter to apply.
